Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Simplify Studio ("we", "us", "our") builds mobile games and applications. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect across all our apps and games, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account data (optional): If you sign in with Google or another provider, we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo to create your account, enable cloud saves, and display your name on leaderboards.

Game/app progress: Your save data and preferences are stored on our servers so you can resume on any device.

Purchase history: When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive a purchase receipt through our payment processor (RevenueCat) to verify and deliver your items. We do not store your payment method or billing details.

Device & usage data: We collect anonymous analytics data such as device type, OS version, app version, session duration, and usage events via Firebase Analytics. This helps us improve our apps. Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports to help us fix issues.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise location / GPS data
  • Contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Data from children under 13 beyond what is necessary for gameplay

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the app/game experience
  • Save and sync your progress across devices
  • Verify in-app purchases and deliver items
  • Display leaderboard rankings
  • Improve performance, fix bugs (via Crashlytics), and plan features
  • Understand usage patterns (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Display ads (when applicable, via Google AdMob)

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (authentication, cloud saves) — Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (sign-in, billing) — Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (purchase management) — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads, when enabled) — Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

Our apps are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is necessary for app functionality. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Advertising

Some of our apps may display ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use anonymous device identifiers to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. We retain your data as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through in-app settings or by emailing us.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all data (via in-app settings or email)
  • Opt out of optional features like sign-in, notifications, or personalized ads
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance.
